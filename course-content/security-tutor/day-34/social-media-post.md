## LinkedIn

12 questions. 9 covered 9 different sessions — cloud IAM, detection engineering, privilege escalation, shared responsibility — and every one landed clean or needed only a light touch. Best checkpoint run yet in this series.

But one of the other 3 questions caught something worth sharing: I got the exact same OAuth/OIDC question wrong a second time, weeks apart, in the same direction (swapped which token proves identity vs. which one grants access).

One miss is noise. Two genuine misses on the same concept is signal — it means a quick verbal correction isn't enough, and it needs a dedicated session, not another quiz question. That's exactly what got scheduled next.

Smaller but related finding: classified S3 as "PaaS" when it's really closer to a fully managed, SaaS-like service — no platform to deploy onto, unlike a true PaaS example like RDS. The underlying substance (zero infrastructure patching on the customer side) was right both times; only the label keeps slipping.

Takeaway: don't just track right vs. wrong — track whether this is the first, second, or third time you've been asked. The pattern across attempts tells you more than any single answer.
